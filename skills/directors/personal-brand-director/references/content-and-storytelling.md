## Storytelling Framework

Every story arc should support one or more of these five threads:

**Growth** — What changed and why it mattered.
**Learning** — What the experience taught. What would be done differently.
**Execution** — What was actually done. Specifics over generalities.
**Responsibility** — Accountability for outcomes. Ownership without excuse.
**Discipline** — The commitment behind the result. The process behind the highlight.

**Story structure (personal brand posts):**
```
Opening     — One specific moment, observation, or truth. No preamble.
Context     — What was actually happening. Ground it in reality.
Tension     — What made it hard, uncertain, or uncomfortable.
Resolution  — What happened. What was decided. What was learned.
Principle   — The transferable insight. Not "do this" — "this is what I found."
Close       — No heavy CTA. Optional: soft direction toward community or offer.
```

## Content Decision Filter

Before publishing anything under the JorDache Darling personal brand:

1. **Authority test:** Does this establish or reinforce expertise and credibility?
2. **Trust test:** Does this build or maintain trust with the target audience?
3. **Positioning test:** Does this move toward institutional figure or back toward internet personality?
4. **Identity test:** Is this consistent with the Sovereign Operator archetype?
5. **Five-year test:** Would a respected investor, client, or partner feel comfortable seeing this in 2031?

If any answer is no or uncertain — revise or hold.

## What Never Gets Published Under JorDache Darling

- Specific return claims or performance numbers (outside qualified, contextualized reporting)
- Political opinions or commentary
- Public criticism of named individuals
- Relationship or personal family details beyond what JorDache voluntarily shares
- Content that could be interpreted as financial advice
- Content that looks like flexing without context (car, watch, cash photos without narrative purpose)
- Any content with the energy of: "look how successful I am" — replace with "here's what I learned"
