## Competition Prep Protocol Framework

### Phase 1 — Base Conditioning (Current → Bali Arrival)
**Goal:** Maximum leanness. Preserve muscle. Establish competition-ready base.

**Training:** PPL 6 days. Volume maintained. Intensity progressive.
**Nutrition:** Caloric deficit — moderate, not aggressive. Protein at ceiling (1g/lb minimum).
Cardio: 3–4 sessions per week (LISS preferred — treadmill incline walk, 45 minutes).

**Tracking:**
- Weekly weight average (not daily — avoid noise)
- Weekly photos: front, back, side
- Strength benchmarks monitored — any significant strength loss triggers nutrition adjustment

### Phase 2 — Competition Prep Proper (Bali — approximately September–November)
**Goal:** Stage-ready conditioning. Visible muscle separation, low body fat, peak performance.

**Training:** Volume maintained or slightly increased. Heavier compound work preserved.
Accessory work shifts to hypertrophy rep ranges (8–15) with shorter rest.
**Nutrition:** More aggressive deficit with refeed structure. Carbohydrate cycling begins.
**Cardio:** Increases to 5–6 sessions per week. Fasted LISS in morning block.

**Tracking:**
- Twice weekly photos
- Circumference measurements: waist, chest, arms, legs — tracked weekly
- Body fat estimate monthly (calipers or DEXA if available in Bali)

### Phase 3 — Peak Week (Final 7 Days Before Competition)
**Goal:** Maximum visual impact on stage. Water and glycogen manipulation.

**Protocol:** Peak week is highly individual — built and refined based on how the body
responds throughout Phase 2. Do not plan peak week specifics until Phase 2 is complete
and the body's response to carbohydrate loading and water manipulation is understood.

**General principles:**
- Sodium manipulation (reduce then reload)
- Water intake adjustment (not elimination)
- Carbohydrate load timed to fill glycogen stores at competition
- Training reduces to maintenance stimulus only — no new fatigue

## Men's Physique — Stage Standards

**Judging criteria (general):**
- Symmetry between upper and lower body
- V-taper (shoulder-to-waist ratio)
- Conditioning (leanness without the extreme of bodybuilding)
- Overall aesthetics and presentation
- Stage presence and confidence

**Attire:** Board shorts (competition standard). No trunks.

**Posing requirements:**
- Front stance
- Back stance
- Quarter turns
- Comparison round

Posing practice begins in Phase 2. Minimum 15 minutes per day in the 8 weeks before competition.
Posing is as important as conditioning — a well-conditioned physique presented poorly loses to
a less-conditioned physique presented well.

## Supplementation Framework

**Non-negotiables:**
- Creatine monohydrate: 5g daily (timing irrelevant — consistency matters)
- Vitamin D3 + K2: 5,000–10,000 IU daily (especially if limited sun exposure)
- Omega-3: 3–4g EPA/DHA daily (anti-inflammatory, recovery, cognitive)
- Magnesium glycinate: 400mg before bed (sleep quality, recovery)

**Performance:**
- Caffeine: pre-training as needed — not exceeding 400mg daily, cycle breaks monthly
- Protein powder: as needed to hit daily protein target — whole food first

**Cut timing:**
Phase 2 includes reviewing whether any cut cycles are appropriate based on
physiological response and competition timeline.

**Never:** Anything that is not legal, declared, or competition-compliant.
