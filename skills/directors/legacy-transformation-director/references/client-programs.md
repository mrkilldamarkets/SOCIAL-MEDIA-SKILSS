## Client Onboarding Process

### Application
- Application form captures: goals, current training history, nutrition habits, lifestyle,
  business/income context, timeline, and why they want to work with Legacy By Darling
- JorDache reviews personally — fit is assessed before any pricing conversation

### Discovery Call (Post-Application Approval)
- 30–45 minutes
- Assess: current body composition baseline / training experience / lifestyle constraints /
  work schedule / nutrition knowledge / compliance history
- Confirm: 3-month minimum understanding / check-in commitment / photography requirement

### Intake Questionnaire (Post-Call, Pre-Start)
Detailed intake covering:
- Training history (years, modalities, injuries)
- Current program (if any)
- Current nutrition (typical day of eating)
- Sleep quality and hours
- Stress levels and lifestyle context
- Body composition goals (specific — not "get in shape")
- Business and daily schedule constraints
- Supplements currently used
- Medical considerations

### Week 1 — Foundation Setup
- Baseline photos: front, back, side (required — no exceptions)
- Baseline measurements: weight, body fat estimate, key measurements
- Program delivered with full exercise descriptions and coaching notes
- Nutrition targets set with explanation (not just numbers)
- Habit stack identified: 3–5 daily non-negotiables for the first month
- Check-in format explained and first check-in scheduled

## Program Design Standards

### Training Philosophy

Objective: not aesthetics alone — capability.

Build: Strength / Conditioning / Mobility / Confidence / Presence / Longevity

**Default split:** Push / Pull / Legs (PPL) — 6 days on, 1 rest
Adjusted based on client schedule, experience level, and recovery capacity.

**Progressive overload principle:** Every week has a measurable target — more weight,
more reps, or better execution quality. Plateau is not acceptable without adjustment.

**Program components per session:**
- Warm-up protocol (not optional)
- Primary compound lifts (2–3 per session)
- Accessory work (3–4 movements)
- Cool-down / mobility (minimum 5 minutes)

### Training Program Format

```
DAY [X] — [PUSH / PULL / LEGS / REST]

WARM-UP (10 minutes):
  [Movement 1] — [Sets × Reps]
  [Movement 2] — [Sets × Reps]

PRIMARY:
  [Exercise] — [Sets × Reps] — [Load target] — [Coaching note]
  [Exercise] — [Sets × Reps] — [Load target] — [Coaching note]

ACCESSORY:
  [Exercise] — [Sets × Reps] — [Coaching note]
  [Exercise] — [Sets × Reps] — [Coaching note]
  [Exercise] — [Sets × Reps] — [Coaching note]

FINISHER (optional):
  [Circuit or conditioning work — 10 minutes max]

COOL-DOWN:
  [Stretching protocol — targeted to session muscles]
```

### Nutrition Framework

Nutrition supports: Performance / Recovery / Body composition / Energy / Hormonal health / Longevity

**Philosophy:** No extreme restriction. No crash approaches. Build sustainable eating infrastructure.

**Macro calculation baseline:**
- Protein: 0.8–1g per lb bodyweight (non-negotiable — always the first target)
- Carbohydrates: adjusted to training intensity and body composition goal
- Fats: minimum 0.4g per lb bodyweight for hormonal health

**Meal structure guidelines:**
- Protein at every meal (minimum 40g per sitting)
- Pre-training: carbohydrate-forward, moderate fat
- Post-training: protein + carbohydrate within 60 minutes
- Evening: protein-forward, lower carbohydrate (context-dependent)

**Foods emphasized:** Whole protein sources, complex carbohydrates, healthy fats,
vegetables at most meals, adequate hydration (bodyweight in lbs ÷ 2 = oz daily minimum)

**Never prescribe:** Extreme caloric deficits / Single-food protocols / Unsustainable elimination diets

## Weekly Check-In System

**Submission day:** Friday (review delivered Saturday)
**Format:** Structured form submission — not open-ended messages

### Check-In Template (Client Submits)

```
WEEK [X] CHECK-IN — [Name]

TRAINING:
  Days completed:      [X of X planned]
  Missed sessions:     [Which + honest reason]
  Energy during training: [1–10]
  Any pain or injury:  [Yes / No — describe if yes]
  Notable progress:    [PRs, improvements, observations]

NUTRITION:
  Daily compliance:    [X of 7 days on target]
  Biggest challenge:   [Honest answer]
  Meals that worked:   [What fueled well]

WEIGHT / MEASUREMENTS:
  Morning weight (avg): [X lbs]
  Weekly change:        [+/- X lbs]

SLEEP:
  Average hours:        [X hrs]
  Quality:              [1–10]

HABITS / NON-NEGOTIABLES:
  [Habit 1]: [X of 7 days]
  [Habit 2]: [X of 7 days]
  [Habit 3]: [X of 7 days]

OVERALL COMPLIANCE:   [1–10]
BIGGEST WIN:          [Honest]
BIGGEST STRUGGLE:     [Honest]
QUESTIONS:            [Anything specific]
```

### Coach Response Format

```
WEEK [X] REVIEW — [Name]

TRAINING: [Specific feedback — what was good, what needs correction]
NUTRITION: [Specific feedback — what to adjust if anything]
ADJUSTMENTS: [Program or nutrition changes for next week, if any]
FOCUS NEXT WEEK: [One to two specific things to prioritize]
OBSERVATION: [One honest observation about their trajectory — positive or corrective]
```

Response delivered within 24 hours of submission. Never delayed.

## Progress Photography Protocol

**Frequency:** Weekly (optional but strongly recommended) / Monthly (required)

**Conditions (non-negotiable for accurate comparison):**

- Same lighting
- Same location
- Same poses: front relaxed, back relaxed, side relaxed
- Same clothing (compression shorts or swimwear)

**Storage:** Supabase Storage with client folder structure
**Access:** Client and JorDache only — never shared without explicit written permission
