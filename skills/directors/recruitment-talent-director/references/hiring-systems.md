# Hiring Systems — Process, Evaluation & Performance

## Role Definition Template

Never open a search without completing this first:

```
ROLE DEFINITION:

Title:                  [Specific, not generic]
Brand / Department:     [Which entity this serves]
Mission:                [One sentence — why this role exists]
Reports To:             [Specific person]

Core Responsibilities:
  1. [Primary — the most important thing this role does]
  2. [Secondary]
  3. [Tertiary]
  (Maximum 5 — if you list 10, you haven't defined the role)

KPIs (How success is measured):
  Primary:    [Most important metric — must be measurable]
  Secondary:  [Supporting metrics]

90-Day Success Looks Like:
  [Specific, observable outcomes — not "settled in" or "learning the role"]

Compensation Range:     $[X] – $[X] per month / project / hour
Structure:              Full-time contractor / Part-time / Project-based

Non-Negotiable Traits:  [3 character traits without which this person fails regardless of skill]
Disqualifying Traits:   [What immediately removes a candidate]
```

## Candidate Evaluation Framework

### Stage 1 — Initial Filter (Resume / Portfolio)

Questions:
- Does their output quality match the standard required?
- Do their results (not tasks) demonstrate what we need?
- Is there evidence of ownership and initiative — or just execution of direction?

Time investment: 5–10 minutes. Move fast. Most candidates are filtered here.

### Stage 2 — Written Assessment

Before any call, send a specific brief or test relevant to the role.

**For editors:** Edit a provided raw clip to brief
**For designers:** Deliver a thumbnail concept for a real video
**For community roles:** Write a response to a difficult community situation
**For research/analyst roles:** Produce a brief market note on XAUUSD

**Assessment criteria:** Quality of output / Attention to detail / How they handle the brief /
Whether they ask good questions or make incorrect assumptions

### Stage 3 — Interview

Structure — 45 minutes maximum:

```
0:00–0:10  Their background — let them tell the story. Listen for ownership language vs. passive language.
0:10–0:20  Specific scenarios: "Tell me about a time you [had to solve X without direction]"
0:20–0:30  Role-specific discussion: what they'd do in the first 30 days
0:30–0:40  Their questions — the quality of their questions reveals more than their answers
0:40–0:45  Expectations alignment and next steps
```

**Listen for:**
- Do they say "I" or "we" when describing their own contributions?
- Do they blame teammates or external factors for failures?
- Do they show curiosity about the mission — or just the role's tasks?
- Are their questions about growth and impact — or just hours and pay?

### Stage 4 — Trial Period (Non-Negotiable)

Every hire begins with a paid trial. No exceptions.

**Duration:** 2–4 weeks for contractor roles / 30–60 days for more integrated roles

**Trial brief:**
```
Trial Objective:    [Specific deliverable or period of work]
Duration:           [X weeks]
Compensation:       $[X] for the trial period
Evaluation Criteria:
  1. [Quality of output]
  2. [Communication and reliability]
  3. [Initiative and problem solving]
  4. [Cultural alignment]
Decision Point:     [Date when full engagement decision is made]
```

**Trial truth:** If there is hesitation at the end of the trial — do not proceed.
Hesitation is a signal. The full engagement will be worse, not better.

## Performance Management System

### Monthly Check-In (Every Active Team Member)

```
MONTHLY REVIEW — [Name] — [Month]

KPI PERFORMANCE:
  [Metric 1]:   [Target] vs [Actual] — [On track / Behind / Ahead]
  [Metric 2]:   [Target] vs [Actual] — [On track / Behind / Ahead]

WHAT'S WORKING:
  [Specific — not generic praise]

WHAT NEEDS TO IMPROVE:
  [Direct — not softened to the point of being unclear]

NEXT MONTH FOCUS:
  [One to two specific areas of development or output]

RETENTION ASSESSMENT:
  [Strong fit / Monitoring / At risk — internal only]
```

### Performance Warning Protocol

If performance is below standard for two consecutive months:

1. Direct conversation — specific, evidence-based, no vague language
2. Written improvement plan with clear metrics and timeline (30 days)
3. Review at 30 days — resolved or exit

**Never tolerate mediocrity hoping it improves without direct intervention.**
Passive tolerance of poor performance harms the people performing well around it.

## Contractor Management Framework

For freelancers and project-based contractors:

```
CONTRACTOR BRIEF:

Name:               [Name]
Role:               [Specific function]
Scope:              [Exactly what they deliver — not "help with design"]
Deliverables:       [Specific files, formats, quantities]
Timeline:           [Deadlines per deliverable]
Revisions:          [How many included]
Rate:               $[X] per [project/hour/month]
Communication:      [How and where — Telegram / Slack / Email]
File Delivery:      [Format and destination]
Ownership:          [All work product is owned by 4x Heist Investments on delivery]
```

**Contractor evaluation (monthly for ongoing):**
- Delivery on time: Y / N
- Quality meets standard: Y / N
- Communication responsive: Y / N
- Proactive problem-solving: Y / N
- Would expand scope: Y / N

Two or more No answers triggers a direct conversation or replacement.

## Leadership Pipeline

Identify high-potential internal talent by watching for:

- Delivering above and beyond the defined scope without being asked
- Solving problems before raising them
- Positive cultural influence on others around them
- Consistent reliability over an extended period (not just during visible moments)
- Curiosity about the broader mission — not just their specific role

**Development path:**
1. Expand scope with increased responsibility
2. Give ownership of a specific initiative (not just a task)
3. Create visibility — include in strategic conversations
4. Formal leadership role when organizational need and readiness align

**Succession planning:** Every critical role should have an identified backup.
If a role has no backup and the person leaves — the business is exposed.
