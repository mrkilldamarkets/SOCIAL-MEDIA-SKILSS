# Member Systems — Onboarding, Journey & Retention

## Member Journey (8 Stages)

Every member has a defined path. The community's job is to move them forward along it.

```
Stage 01 — Application        Join and access granted
Stage 02 — Onboarding         First 14 days. Orientation, goals set, accountability started.
Stage 03 — Integration        Actively participating. Questions asked. Journal started.
Stage 04 — Education          Modules being worked through. Concepts being applied.
Stage 05 — Consistency        30+ day journal streak. Process adherence. Execution improving.
Stage 06 — Funded Trader      Active prop firm challenge or first funded account.
Stage 07 — Operator Status    Consistent funded profits. Multiple income streams. Phase 04 complete.
Stage 08 — Community Leader   Contributing back. Mentoring others. Ambassador potential.
```

**Discord role alignment:**
- Stages 1–3: "4x Prophet Member" or "HTBB Member"
- Stage 5: "Consistent Operator"
- Stage 6: "Funded Trader"
- Stage 7: "Operator"
- Stage 8: "Community Ambassador" or "HTBB Legend" (nomination-based)

## Onboarding Flow (Days 1–14)

### Day 0 — Access Granted
- Discord role assigned → welcome DM sent → resource guide delivered
- Staff posts custom welcome in #introduce-yourself (see welcome post format below)

### Day 1 — Orientation
- Introduction post required in #introduce-yourself (template below)
- Goals post required in #goals
- Orientation video and checklist sent via DM

### Day 3 — First Check-In
- DM from staff: "How are you settling in? Have you posted your intro yet?"
- If no activity: gentle nudge — not pressure

### Day 7 — Onboarding Call (Tier 01)
- 30-minute call with Noah or staff
- Agenda: current level / recommended starting module / accountability setup / expectations

### Day 14 — Two-Week Review
- DM check-in: course progress, community activity, first questions
- If zero activity: "What's getting in the way?" — human response required, not automated

## Accountability Framework

**Accountability channel cadence:**
- Sunday: "This week I will [specific commitment]" — posted by member
- Friday: "Results: [what happened] — [honest reflection]" — posted by member

**Staff role in accountability:**
- React to Sunday posts with ✅ to acknowledge
- Comment on Friday posts when someone shows notable discipline or honest reflection
- Never chase members who don't post — this is a self-selected accountability system

**Accountability partner system:**
- On onboarding call, match new member with a member in Stage 4–5 as an accountability partner
- Partner checks in weekly, not daily — this is support, not babysitting

```
Prop Firm Status
Accountability Partner
Engagement Score (1–5, updated monthly)
Retention Risk Flag (Green / Yellow / Red)
Notes
```

### Retention Risk Tiers

| Risk | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Active last 7 days, posting regularly | No action needed |
| Yellow | No activity 14+ days | Automated DM — warm, not salesy |
| Red | No activity 30+ days | Personal message from Noah or staff |
| Lost | No response to outreach + no activity 45 days | Exit, cancellation expected |

### Re-engagement Message (Yellow)

```
"Hey [Name] — haven't seen you around the community lately. Completely normal to have busy
stretches. What's been going on? Is there anything I can help with or any questions I can answer?
We're here."
```

Not a pitch. Not a "don't cancel" message. Genuine.

## Leadership Pipeline Development

Identify potential Community Leaders at Stage 6–7 by watching for:
- Consistent quality posts in #trade-reviews and #trade-ideas
- Helping other members in #questions without being asked
- Public accountability and honest reflection in #accountability
- Positive culture reinforcement (shutting down negativity, encouraging others)

**Development path:**
1. Informal recognition — feature in #member-spotlights
2. Invite to be accountability partner for new members
3. Offer Community Ambassador role (moderation support, culture reinforcement)
4. Future: coaching or mentor role within HTBB structure

**Leadership is earned through contribution — never assigned by tenure or payment.**
