# Post-Production — Editing, Color, Music & Asset Management

## Editing Philosophy

Focus on: Story / Emotion / Narrative / Progression / Transformation

**The pacing rule:** Fast enough to retain attention. Slow enough to feel premium.

This means:
- No shot held longer than its story purpose requires
- No jump cuts that feel chaotic or disrespectful of the viewer's attention
- No trend editing (whip pans, zoom cuts, flash transitions as default)
- Breathing room exists in premium content — silence and stillness are tools, not failures

---

## Editing Style by Format

### Long-Form YouTube (Vlog / Documentary)

**Pacing:** Deliberate. Editorial. Every cut is intentional.

**Principles:**
- Story drives the cut — not the music
- Let moments breathe when emotion or insight is peaking
- Use jump cuts only when time needs to compress, not to add energy
- B-roll covers edits — not hides them
- Audio is as important as the picture (room tone, ambient sound, breathing)

**Sequence structure:**
```
Cold open (in the action)
↓ Establish context with B-roll
↓ Primary talking head or narration sequence
↓ B-roll to cover or extend thought
↓ Secondary scene or moment
↓ Repeat until climax
↓ Lesson or reflection beat
↓ Close with a forward-looking moment or CTA
```

**Common errors to avoid:**
- Starting the edit with the first shot captured (almost never the best opening)
- Overusing talking head with no visual support
- Letting music dictate the emotional arc instead of the story
- Not using silence — every good documentary has quiet moments

---

### Short-Form Reels / TikTok

**Pacing:** Fast in the first three seconds. Controlled after.

**First 3 seconds:** Hook is visual AND verbal simultaneously.
The opening frame is the thumbnail — it must be compelling before anything is said.

**Reel structure:**
```
Frame 1:     Hook shot — visual pattern interrupt or strong image
0:00–0:03:  Hook line — spoken or text overlay. Creates the open loop.
0:03–0:15:  Core content — education, insight, or documentary moment
0:15–0:25:  Application or resolution
0:25–0:30:  CTA — single word or phrase
```

**What makes a reel work:**
- Hook that creates a question the viewer needs answered
- Pacing that matches the energy of the content (slower for wisdom, faster for execution)
- B-roll that supports the spoken track — not random cutaways
- Text overlay used sparingly — only for key phrases or hooks, not transcription

## Color Grading Direction

**Target look:** Luxury / Cinematic / Depth / Clean highlights / Controlled shadows / Premium feel

**Color philosophy:**
- Shadows should have depth — not crushed to pure black
- Highlights should feel clean — not blown out or clinical
- Skin tones are non-negotiable — always accurate, never orange or green
- Color temperature: slight warmth in lifestyle/travel content / cooler tone in trading/business content

**Grade references:**
- Travel: warm, golden, expansive — think luxury travel documentary
- Trading/business: cooler, precise, institutional — think financial documentary
- Gym/fitness: high contrast, gritty but controlled — power and effort visible
- Evening/reflection: moody, deeper shadows, soft warmth

**LUT guidance:**
- Build a signature LUT for each content category above
- Apply consistently — brand recognition comes from visual consistency over time
- Never use trending LUT packs that date the content immediately

## Music Direction

**Music supports:** Emotion / Momentum / Narrative / Tension / Progress

**Music never:** Overpowers dialogue / Distracts from story / Defines the edit's pacing entirely

### Music by Content Category

**Operator Diaries / Vlog:**
- Ambient, understated, minimal
- Orchestral or electronic — not commercial pop
- Should feel like it belongs in a Netflix documentary
- References: Hans Zimmer ambient work, Nils Frahm, Ólafur Arnalds, Lo-Fi cinematic

**Trading Intelligence / Education:**
- Subtle, focused, not distracting
- Should support concentration, not compete with information
- References: minimal electronic, single piano motifs, barely-there ambient

**Travel / Movement sequences:**
- Can be more dynamic when cutting to a destination arrival
- Building energy as the location establishes
- References: modern cinematic builds, ambient electronic with forward momentum

**Gym / Training:**
- More intensity acceptable — but not club music
- Should feel like controlled aggression, not hype
- References: cinematic percussion, industrial ambient, orchestral percussion

**Licensing:**
- Use royalty-free music from premium libraries (Artlist, Musicbed, Epidemic Sound)
- Never use trending songs — they date the content and create copyright issues
- Build a personal music library organized by mood and use case

## Audio Production Standards

Poor audio kills premium content faster than poor visuals.

**Non-negotiables:**
- Lavalier mic for all talking head and long-form content
- Shotgun mic for environmental and B-roll audio capture
- No wind noise — use a windscreen, always
- Room tone recorded at every location (30 seconds minimum) for post-production use
- Audio levels: dialogue at -12 to -6 dB, music at -24 to -18 dB under dialogue

**Audio treatment:**
- Noise reduction on all dialogue tracks before any other processing
- EQ: roll off below 80Hz on voice (removes room rumble)
- Compression on dialogue: moderate, transparent — consistent level without pumping
- Music ducking: music drops -6 to -10 dB under dialogue, restores in non-dialogue sections

## Asset Management System

### File Naming Convention

```
[Date]_[Category]_[Location]_[Description]_[Version].[ext]
Examples:
  20260612_Trading_DaNang_ChartAnalysis_v1.mp4
  20260615_Travel_Airport_WalkingSequence_A.mp4
  20260618_Fitness_Hotel_MorningWarmup_v2.mp4
```

### Export Standards

**YouTube (Long-form):**
- 4K (3840×2160) or 1080p (1920×1080) minimum
- H.264 or H.265 codec
- 50–60 Mbps bitrate
- Color space: Rec.709

**Instagram Reels / TikTok:**
- 1080×1920 (9:16 vertical)
- H.264
- 30 Mbps minimum

**Instagram Feed / Thumbnail:**
- 1080×1080 or 1080×1350
- JPEG 95%+ quality

### Backup Protocol

**3-2-1 rule:** 3 copies / 2 different formats / 1 offsite
- Primary: Working drive (NVMe SSD)
- Secondary: External drive backup
- Tertiary: Cloud storage (Google Drive or similar)

Raw footage is never deleted. Storage is an investment. A shot that doesn't fit today
may be exactly the B-roll needed for a campaign six months from now.
