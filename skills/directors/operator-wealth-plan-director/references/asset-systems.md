**Asset growth principle:**
Capture first. Post later. The vault should always be 60+ days ahead of the content calendar.

## Asset 7 — Operator Reserve

**Goal:** $100,000 untouchable

**The three rules of the operator reserve:**
1. Never traded
2. Never invested
3. Never touched

**Purpose:** Eliminate scarcity pressure.

Most bad decisions are made because survival pressure exists. When you need the next trade to work, the next client to sign, the next month's revenue to cover expenses — you are operating from scarcity. Scarcity degrades decision quality.

The reserve removes survival pressure. Every decision after the reserve is established is made from abundance, not desperation. This alone is worth more than any trading edge.

**Building the reserve:**
- Priority 1 in capital allocation once operational expenses are covered
- Built before lifestyle expansion
- Kept in separate account — never co-mingled with operational capital
- Mental model: this money does not exist for any current opportunity

## Operator Wealth Allocation Model

When new capital enters the system, allocate intentionally:

```
Priority 1: Taxes (set aside before spending anything)
Priority 2: Debt elimination
Priority 3: Operator Reserve (until $100K is reached — then maintain)
Priority 4: Business reinvestment (highest ROI opportunities first)
Priority 5: Asset accumulation (HTBB infrastructure / media / real estate)
Priority 6: Lifestyle

Lifestyle spending comes after assets. Not before.
```

**The test for any capital allocation:**
Does this purchase/investment appear as an asset in the eight sovereign assets? If not — it is consumption, not allocation. Consumption can happen after asset targets are met.
