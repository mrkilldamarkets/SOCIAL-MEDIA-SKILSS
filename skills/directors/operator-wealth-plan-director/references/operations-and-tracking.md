## Media Vault Protocol

Always think like a media owner, not a content creator.

**The daily capture habit:**
Before leaving any location, asking: "Did I add something to the vault today?"

**Capture priority list (ranked by leverage):**
1. Trading sessions with live chart (evergreen education content)
2. Gym and training (discipline and competition prep narrative)
3. Airports and travel transitions (operator mobility narrative)
4. Hotel and workspace setups (environment design proof)
5. City environments and architecture (lifestyle documentation)
6. Planning sessions and strategy moments (behind-the-build content)
7. Meals and nutrition prep (Legacy By Darling / operator health content)

**Technical standard:**
- Minimum 1080p for all video — 4K where possible
- RAW or highest quality for photos
- Audio captured separately for talking head content
- Every file named: [Date]_[Location]_[Category]_[Description]

**Vault targets by category:**

| Category | Current Target |
|---|---|
| Photos total | 1,000+ |
| B-roll clips | 300+ |
| Long-form videos | 50+ |
| Published Reels | 100+ |
| Talking head footage | 20+ hours raw |

**Organization system:**
- Primary folder: Location
- Secondary folder: Date
- Tertiary tag: Theme (Trading / Training / Travel / Business / Lifestyle / Brand)
- Usability rating on every file: A (post-ready) / B (usable with edit) / C (archive only)

**The 60-day rule:** The vault should always contain at least 60 days of post-able content that could be deployed without recording anything new. This is the media equivalent of the operator reserve — protection against circumstances (illness, travel, high-focus execution periods) that prevent active capture.

## Strategic Alliance Framework

**Goal:** Build relationships with 3 elite operators.

**The criteria test:**
Would you trust them with:
- Capital
- Opportunities
- Business
- Family

If not: they are acquaintances, not allies.

Quality over quantity. Three aligned, trusted relationships are worth more than one hundred network contacts.

**Current alliances to cultivate:**
- Sultan (Blue Marlin discretionary co-operator — existing)
- Noah Bush (HTBB co-founder — existing)
- [Third alliance — identify through operator communities, masterminds, events in Bali/Singapore/Bahamas]

**Alliance building protocol:**
- Identify through the rooms they're already in (events, masterminds, high-quality communities)
- Give value before asking for anything
- Build trust through small demonstrations over time — not single interactions
- The alliance relationship deepens when both parties have been honest during a difficult moment, not just during good times

**Alliance vs. network distinction:**
- Network: people who know what you do
- Alliance: people you would call if something went wrong
- Build the alliance first. The network grows automatically from the alliance's credibility.
