## Content Integration Strategy

Affiliate recommendations should be **integrated, not injected.**

**Integration by content type:**

| Content Type | Integration Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube tutorials | Natural mention when the tool is genuinely relevant to the content | As relevant |
| Trading education | Prop firm mention at the point when challenge strategy is discussed | 2x/week max |
| Resource recommendations | Dedicated "tools I use" content (quarterly) | Quarterly |
| Community posts (Telegram/Discord) | Only when directly answering a question | On request |
| Email sequences | Included in relevant educational sequences where it adds genuine value | 1x per sequence |

**Alpha Capital integration schedule:**
- YouTube: minimum 1 mention per video in trading education content (natural, not forced)
- YouTube description: always includes Alpha Capital link in the description template
- Instagram: included in prop firm content — not every post
- Telegram: included in prop firm challenge discussions

## Affiliate Asset Creation

Build evergreen content assets that generate passive affiliate revenue:

**Asset types:**

| Asset | Description | Update Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Prop firm comparison guide | Comprehensive breakdown of top firms with honest assessment | Quarterly |
| Broker comparison guide | Evaluation of brokers for trading and funded accounts | Semi-annual |
| "Tools I use" page | Website page listing all recommended tools with context | As partnerships change |
| Resource library | In-community document listing all partner resources | Monthly |
| Review videos (YouTube) | In-depth honest reviews of prop firms and tools | Per major partner |

**Asset creation standard:** Every comparison and review must be honest, including negatives. The moment a recommendation reads like advertising — it loses all conversion power. Honest reviews with real assessment convert better and protect trust.

```
PORTFOLIO SUMMARY:
  Total revenue:              $[X]
  Recurring revenue:          $[X] (subscription-based partners)
  One-time revenue:           $[X]
  Revenue concentration risk: Top partner = [X]% of total
  Partners at risk:           [Any partners with declining trend or business concerns]
```

**Concentration rule:** No single affiliate partner should represent more than 50% of total affiliate revenue. If Alpha Capital exceeds this — actively develop secondary revenue streams.

## Recurring Revenue Strategy

**Prioritize partners with recurring commission structures:**

1. **Subscription software** — Earn monthly commission as long as the referred customer stays subscribed
2. **Revenue share brokers** — Earn per lot traded by referred clients (BlackBull: $8/lot)
3. **Performance-based prop firms** — Some offer recurring structures as traders re-challenge
4. **Managed account referral fees** — Ongoing percentage of management fees on referred capital

**Recurring revenue target:** Build affiliate income such that $X/month is recurring regardless of new promotion activity. This is the passive baseline that makes the affiliate ecosystem an asset rather than an effort.

## Partner Negotiation Framework

Always negotiate from value — the audience is an asset worth fighting for.

**Negotiation priorities:**
1. Higher revenue share (push for 2–5% above standard)
2. Exclusive discount code for community (increases conversion AND audience value)
3. Co-branded campaigns (increases both parties' reach)
4. Custom offer structure (something the community can't get elsewhere)
5. Dedicated account manager (faster issue resolution, better communication)

**What we offer in negotiation:**
- Engaged, high-intent audience (traders actively seeking prop firms / tools)
- Content integration, not just a link
- Long-term relationship (not switching for marginal commission improvement)
- Brand credibility (Sovereign Operator positioning attracts serious traders)
