# Brand Risk Profiles — Per-Entity Risk Assessment

## Blue Marlin Securities Risk Profile

**Highest risk entity** — manages third-party capital. Mistakes here have the most severe consequences.

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Investor concentration | High if <5 investors | Actively diversify investor base; no single investor >30% AUM |
| Broker dependence (BlackBull) | Medium | Document process for migrating to alternative broker if needed |
| Strategy dependence | Medium | HEIST PROPHECY + ORACLE are separate; if one fails, other continues |
| Reporting failure | High | Monthly report delivery is non-negotiable; automate preparation |
| Drawdown-triggered withdrawals | Medium | Proactive communication protocol mitigates; see IR skill |
| Regulatory exposure | Medium | Ensure LPOA documentation is current; work with attorney |
| Reputation damage | Critical | One major public failure destroys years of brand building |

**Blue Marlin risk principle:** Everything is secondary to investor trust.
A 20% drawdown communicated transparently is survivable. A 10% drawdown hidden is not.

## Financial Risk Thresholds (Ecosystem-Wide)

```
RESERVE MINIMUMS (TRIGGER IMMEDIATE ACTION IF BREACHED):
  Operational reserve:    Below 2 months → RED FLAG → reduce all discretionary spend
  Emergency reserve:      Below 1.5 months → CRITICAL → revenue recovery focus only
  Opportunity capital:    Below $5,000 → pause all non-essential capital deployment

REVENUE CONCENTRATION TRIGGERS:
  Any source > 60%:      Immediate diversification plan required
  Any source > 70%:      Stop all new initiatives until that source is below 60%

BLUE MARLIN AUM TRIGGERS:
```
