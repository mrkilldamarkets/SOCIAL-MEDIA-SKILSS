# Content Systems — Reels, Hooks, Carousels & Stories

## Reel Framework

Reels are discovery engines — not sales content. Every reel drives reach, attention, and trust.

```
SECONDS 0–3:    HOOK
  → Pattern interrupt or strong opening statement
  → Create the open loop (a question the viewer needs answered)
  → Visual must be compelling before audio is heard (many watch muted)

SECONDS 3–20:   EDUCATION / CONTENT
  → Deliver the core value
  → One clear idea — not three
  → Specific over generic (numbers, steps, named frameworks)

SECONDS 20–28:  INSIGHT
  → The operator angle — what makes this perspective unique
  → The thing they haven't heard before

SECONDS 28–30:  CTA
  → One action: "Comment [word]" / "Follow for more" / "DM me [word]"
  → Never multiple CTAs — one door only
```

## Hook Library (By Category)

**Curiosity hooks:**
- "Most traders never figure this out..."
- "The thing no one tells you about prop firm trading..."
- "I've been doing this for [X] years and only recently realized..."
- "This single insight changed how I approach every trade."

**Pain hooks:**
- "If your trading is inconsistent, this is probably why."
- "You're losing money because of this — and you don't know it."
- "The reason most people never get funded."
- "Why discipline won't fix your trading."

**Contrarian hooks:**
- "Profitable trading is boring. Here's why that's good news."
- "Stop trying to predict the market. Do this instead."
- "The trading advice everyone gives is wrong."
- "Working harder on your trading is making you worse."

**Identity hooks:**
- "Operators don't trade like this..."
- "The difference between a trader and an operator."
- "If you're still doing [X], you're not an operator yet."
- "What funded traders actually do differently."

**Proof hooks:**
- "Here's what [X months] of consistent trading taught me."
- "I went from [X] to [Y]. Here's the actual framework."
- "This is the only system I've used for [X] years."

**Rule:** Every hook must be completable in 3 seconds of screen time or less.
Test: read it aloud at a moderate pace — if it takes more than 3 seconds, cut it.

## Carousel System

**10-slide framework (standard educational carousel):**

```
Slide 01 — PATTERN INTERRUPT
  Text: The hook. 5–8 words. Curiosity gap or bold claim.
  Visual: High contrast. Minimal. Strong.
  Purpose: Stop the scroll.

Slide 02 — PROBLEM
  Text: The situation they're in. Their language, not ours.
  Visual: Can be simpler — the words carry the slide.

Slide 03 — CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE
  Text: Why this happens. The insight they're missing.
  Visual: Optional supporting diagram or minimal graphic.

Slides 04–07 — EDUCATION / STEPS
  Text: The framework. One idea per slide. Specific and actionable.
  Visual: Step number prominent (gold), supporting visual.
  Rule: Each step should be immediately applicable — not theoretical.

Slide 08 — COMMON MISTAKES
  Text: 2–3 specific mistakes people make with this concept.
  Visual: Can use contrast (subtle red for mistake indicators).

Slide 09 — SUMMARY
  Text: The distilled lesson. One powerful sentence.
  Visual: Clean — let the words breathe.

Slide 10 — CTA
  Text: One action. Direct. "DM me [word]" or "Follow for [benefit]"
  Visual: Minimal. No competing elements.
```

**Carousel copy rules:**
- Maximum 30 words per slide (most slides should be under 20)
- Every slide should work standalone — someone may screenshot any slide
- Swipe cue on slide 1: small arrow or "Swipe →" in bottom corner
- Consistent branding placement: same position on every slide

## Story Strategy

**Stories build relationships. Feed builds authority.**

Stories should feel more human — less polished than feed content.

**Story content mix (weekly):**

| Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Behind the scenes | 3–4x/week | Authenticity and connection |
| Progress update | 2–3x/week | Real-time narrative |
| Question/Poll | 2x/week | Engagement + audience intelligence |
| Market insight (quick) | 1–2x/week | Authority reinforcement |
| Community win highlight | 1–2x/week | Social proof |
| CTA / Link story | 2–3x/week | Conversion |

**Story CTA timing:** Place CTA stories after 2–3 value stories — not as the first story.
Earn the swipe-up or link tap; don't demand it.

## Virality Framework

Virality is not luck. It is engineering the conditions for sharing.

**The four virality conditions:**

1. **Relevance** — Does this speak directly to a specific person's current situation?
   The more specific, the more it feels written for them personally.

2. **Emotion** — Does this create a strong feeling?
   Curiosity, recognition, frustration, hope, or identity alignment.
   Neutral content is never shared.

3. **Clarity** — Can the value be understood in 3 seconds?
   If it requires explanation to be good, it won't spread.

4. **Shareability** — Does sharing this make the sharer look smart, helpful, or on-brand?
   People share what reflects well on them.

**Shareability test:** "Would someone screenshot this and send it to a friend with 'this is us' or 'you need to see this'?"
If yes — this content will spread. If no — it's good content but not viral content.
