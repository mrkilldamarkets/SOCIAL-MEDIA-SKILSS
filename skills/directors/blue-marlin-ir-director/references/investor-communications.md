## FAQ Response Framework

### "How is risk managed?"

"Risk management in Blue Marlin Securities operates at three levels.

Position level: every trade has a defined stop loss before entry. We do not move stops against
our favor. Position sizing is calculated to limit single-trade exposure to [X]% of account value.

Account level: there are firm-wide maximum drawdown thresholds. If the strategy approaches
those thresholds, trading activity reduces automatically.

Operational level: performance is reviewed daily. Any deviation from strategy parameters
is addressed immediately.

Your capital remains in your own broker account at all times. We have trading authority
only — you retain full visibility and withdrawal rights."

---

### "What happens during drawdowns?"

"Drawdowns are a normal part of any trading operation. What matters is how they are managed
and communicated.

During a drawdown you will receive proactive communication from me — not silence. I will
explain what is happening, why, and how the strategy is responding. You will not discover
a significant drawdown for the first time in a monthly report.

At the account level, our risk framework includes maximum drawdown thresholds. If those
are approached, trading reduces. Capital preservation is always the first priority.

If at any point you have concerns about a drawdown period, I am available for a direct
conversation."

---

### "How often do I receive reports?"

"Monthly, in the first week of each calendar month. The report covers performance, risk
metrics, market commentary, and forward outlook.

Quarterly, you receive a more comprehensive review of performance, risk, and strategy
development.

In addition, if a significant market event or drawdown situation occurs mid-month, I will
communicate directly without waiting for the scheduled report."

---

### "How liquid is my capital?"

"Your capital is held in your own trading account at BlackBull Markets. Blue Marlin Securities
does not hold your funds. We operate through a Limited Power of Attorney which authorizes
trading activity only.

Withdrawal requests are processed through your broker directly. Standard processing time is
[X] business days. There is no lock-up period. However, withdrawals during open positions
require coordination to ensure positions are managed properly."

---

### "How are fees calculated?"

"Fees are performance-based and calculated at the end of each calendar month.

For algorithmic strategies: [30]% of net profits for the month + [5]% annual management
fee charged monthly ([X]% per month).

For discretionary strategies: [50]% of net profits for the month.

Fees are only charged on growth above the high water mark. If the account is in drawdown
from a previous high, no performance fees apply until that level is recovered and exceeded.

There are no upfront fees, no subscription fees, and no exit fees."

---

### "How is performance measured?"

"Performance is measured net of all fees against the high water mark.

The high water mark means we only earn performance fees on new profits — never on recovering
previous losses. This aligns our incentives directly with yours.

Monthly returns are reported as percentage of account equity at the start of the period.
YTD performance is the compounded result of monthly returns since January 1st or account
inception, whichever is more recent."
