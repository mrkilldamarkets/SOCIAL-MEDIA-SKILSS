# Reporting System — Monthly & Quarterly Standards

## Reporting Philosophy

Reports are not performance summaries. Reports are relationship tools.

A well-crafted report during a losing month keeps an investor. A poorly crafted report during
a winning month creates doubt. The writing, design, and tone matter as much as the numbers.

**Delivery standard:** On time, every month. No exceptions. No delays without prior notice.
A late report without communication is a trust violation — not an administrative inconvenience.

---

## Monthly Report — Full Structure

### Section 01 — Executive Summary

One paragraph. Three to five sentences maximum.

Cover: Month's net performance / Key market theme / One sentence on risk management /
One sentence on positioning going forward.

Write this last. It should synthesize everything below into plain language an investor can
share with their accountant or spouse without confusion.

```
Example:
"[Month] saw XAUUSD trading in a defined range through the first three weeks before a sharp
extension in the final week driven by [macro driver]. The Heist V7 strategy captured [X]% of
that move, closing the month at +[X]% net of fees. Drawdown remained within defined parameters
throughout. We enter [Next Month] with [brief positioning note]."
```

---

### Section 02 — Performance Data

Present all figures net of fees. Always.

```
Monthly Return:           [X]% net
YTD Return:               [X]% net
Peak-to-Trough Drawdown:  [X]% (period: [dates])
Current Drawdown:         [X]% from peak (or "At new high water mark")
Win Rate (month):         [X]%
Average Trade Duration:   [X] hours/days
Number of Trades:         [X]
Largest Single Loss:      [X]% of account
```

**Charting standard:** Performance chart covers rolling 12 months minimum.
Dark background. Gold equity line. Subtle drawdown shading in deep blue.
No retail-style broker screenshots. No MT4/MT5 account statements as the primary visual.

---

### Section 03 — Market Commentary

Three to five paragraphs. Institutional editorial tone.

Structure:
1. What happened in the primary market (XAUUSD) this month
2. The macro drivers behind the move (DXY, real rates, geopolitical, central bank)
3. How those conditions affected strategy execution
4. What the strategy did well / where it faced friction

Tone reference: Financial Times market letter. Calm, factual, analytical.

**Critical rule:** Never spin bad months. If conditions were difficult — say so directly.
"This was a challenging month for the strategy due to [specific reason]. Here is how we responded."
Honest context builds more trust than manufactured optimism.

---

### Section 04 — Risk Management

Structured, not narrative. Investors want to see the system is functioning.

```
Risk Framework Status:     Within defined parameters / [Exception + explanation if not]
Maximum Drawdown Limit:    [X]% (firm-wide threshold)
Current Exposure:          [X]% of capital deployed
Hedging / Protection:      [If applicable]
Risk Adjustments This Month: [Any changes to sizing, stops, or exposure — and why]
Operational Notes:         [Platform updates, broker issues, strategy adjustments]
```

---

### Section 05 — Forward Outlook

Not a prediction. A positioning statement.

Cover:
- Current market environment classification (trending / ranging / volatile / transitional)
- Key events in the coming month (FOMC, CPI, major geopolitical developments)
- How the strategy is positioned entering the period
- What would change the positioning (invalidation conditions)

End with: "We continue to prioritize capital preservation with selective execution in
high-probability conditions."

## Quarterly Review — Full Structure

Delivered within the first week of each new quarter. More comprehensive than the monthly report.

### Performance Review
- Quarterly return (net) and YTD comparison
- Quarter vs. previous quarter performance
- Benchmark context (e.g., XAUUSD spot performance for the quarter)
- Rolling 12-month performance chart

### Capital Growth
- Account value progression (visual — chart preferred)
- Compounding illustration if account has grown
- High water mark status

### Risk Review
- Largest drawdown of the quarter and recovery
- Risk-adjusted return metric
- How risk management framework performed under stress

### Operational Updates
- Any changes to strategy parameters
- Technology or platform developments
- Team or structural updates

### Research Developments
- Any significant additions to the research framework
- Macro themes being monitored going into next quarter

### Strategic Initiatives
- What Blue Marlin is building / developing (without revealing proprietary mechanics)
- Any product or service enhancements relevant to the investor

### Investor Q&A
- Address the two or three most common questions received that quarter
- If no questions were received — address what investors typically ask at this stage

### Forward Plans
- Objectives for the coming quarter
- Market themes being monitored
- Risk environment assessment

## Report Design Standards

**Visual identity:** Blue Marlin Securities design system (see blue-marlin-creative-director skill)
- Background: Deep Ocean Blue (#071A35)
- Text: White (#FFFFFF) headlines / rgba(255,255,255,0.80) body
- Accent: Gold (#D4AF37) for section markers, key metrics, dividers
- Charts: Dark background, gold primary line, no chart junk
- Layout: Generous whitespace. Single column for narrative. Two-column for data tables.

**What it should feel like:** Family office reporting. Private bank letter. Not a broker statement.

**What it must never look like:**
- MT4/MT5 account screenshots as primary exhibit
- Social media graphics or Canva templates
- Retail trading broker report layouts
- Marketing brochure design

**Format:** PDF. Filename: `BlueMarlin_[Strategy]_Report_[MonthYear].pdf`
