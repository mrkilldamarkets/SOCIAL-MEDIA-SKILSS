## Objection Handling Framework

**Approach:** Clarify / Educate / Provide context / Reduce uncertainty / Allow investors to decide.

Never pressure. Never argue. Never force.

---

### "What are your guaranteed returns?"

"We don't offer guaranteed returns — and any managed account that does is misrepresenting the risk.
What we offer is a documented process, transparent reporting, and a risk framework designed to protect
capital first. Returns are the outcome of consistent execution — they can't be guaranteed because
markets can't be guaranteed."

---

### "What happens if I lose money?"

"Drawdowns are a normal part of any trading operation. What matters is how they're managed.
Our risk framework defines maximum drawdown thresholds, and the MAM structure means your capital
stays in your own broker account — we only have limited power to execute trades. You retain full
visibility and withdrawal rights at all times."

---

### "Your fees seem high."

"The fee structure is performance-based — we only earn when you earn. There's no management fee
on strategies that aren't growing your account. A 30% performance fee on consistent growth is a
fraction of what most hedge funds charge. If the strategy doesn't perform, neither do we."

---

### "I've been burned by managed accounts before."

"That's worth understanding. Can you tell me what happened?"

[Listen fully.]

"What you're describing is [name the specific failure — poor risk management / lack of transparency /
no reporting / misrepresentation]. Here's how Blue Marlin operates differently: [specific contrast
points]. I'm not asking you to trust me based on words — I'm asking you to evaluate the structure,
the reporting, and the track record and make your own judgment."

---

### "I need to think about it."

"Of course. Take all the time you need — this decision should be comfortable.
Is there specific information that would help you evaluate it more clearly?
I'd rather answer questions now than have you sitting with uncertainty."

[If no specific blocker: set a light follow-up at 5–7 days, not 24 hours.]

## Monthly Report Standards

Every monthly report delivered to investors covers eight sections:

```
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
   Net performance for the month. One paragraph. Plain language.

2. PERFORMANCE DATA
   Monthly return (net of fees) / YTD return / Peak-to-trough drawdown /
   Sharpe or risk-adjusted metric (where applicable)

3. MARKET ENVIRONMENT
   Context for the month. What drove price. What the macro backdrop was.
   How conditions affected strategy execution.

4. RISK METRICS
   Maximum drawdown in period / Current drawdown from peak /
   Win rate / Average trade duration / Largest single-day loss

5. STRATEGY COMMENTARY
   What the strategy did this month. Why. What deviated from expectations and why.
   Honest if conditions were difficult.

6. TRADE HIGHLIGHTS
   1–3 notable trades with brief thesis. Not a full breakdown — just context.

7. OUTLOOK
   Market environment for the coming month. How strategy is positioned.
   No predictions — probabilities and positioning.

8. OPERATIONAL NOTES
   Any changes to strategy parameters / Risk adjustments / Platform updates /
   Anything the investor should know operationally.
```

**Report design:** Blue Marlin visual standard (see blue-marlin-creative-director skill).
Dark background. Gold accents. Institutional formatting. Never a template PDF.

**Delivery:** On time. Every month. No exceptions. Delayed reports destroy trust faster than
a losing month ever could.
