# Partnership Strategy & Capital Network Development

## Partnership Philosophy

Capital networks compound. One strong referral partner can introduce more qualified investors
than six months of content marketing. Protect every relationship accordingly.

---

## Target Partner Categories

### Tier 1 — Direct Capital Introducers

**Wealth Advisors & IFAs**
- Clients already trust them to direct capital
- Blue Marlin offers an alternative/alternative allocation play
- Relationship requires: credibility, documentation, regulatory clarity
- Approach: institutional — lead with IIM, track record, structure

**Family Office Intermediaries**
- Access to ultra-HNW capital
- Require highest standards of documentation and reporting
- Long relationship timeline (6–18 months before first allocation)
- Worth the patience — one family office relationship changes the AUM curve permanently

**Entrepreneur Networks & Business Communities**
- High earners with capital to deploy outside core business
- Less formal than family offices — faster decision cycle
- Relationship-driven — JorDache's personal brand accelerates trust
- Target: masterminds, private business networks, professional communities

### Tier 2 — Referral Partners

**Broker Partners (BlackBull and others)**
- Natural alignment — more funded accounts benefits both parties
- Potential for formal IB or referral arrangement
- Provide: documentation, track record, clear value proposition

**Complementary Service Providers**
- Accountants, attorneys, and financial planners serving HNW clients
- They don't manage capital — they introduce it
- Relationship approach: educational and patient. Earn trust before asking for anything.

**Content Creators & Educators in Adjacent Spaces**
- Finance, business, entrepreneurship, wealth content creators
- Audience overlap with Blue Marlin's target investor profile
- Approach: collaboration or affiliate introduction arrangement
- Filter: must pass the five-year credibility test

## Partner Onboarding Framework

### Step 1 — Discovery
Understand the partner's audience, clients, and typical capital profile.
Never pitch Blue Marlin until you understand who they're connected to.

### Step 2 — Education
Provide the partner with:
- Strategy Fact Sheets for each offering
- Investor Information Memorandum
- Sample monthly report (redacted)
- Brief video or call walkthrough — not a sales pitch, an education

### Step 3 — Alignment Confirmation
"Does this seem like something your clients / audience would find relevant?"
Let them self-qualify the fit. Never pressure.

### Step 4 — Formalize (If Warranted)
For consistent referral partners:
- Document the referral arrangement (fee or reciprocal value)
- Provide a referral tracking link or code
- Set expectations on communication and reporting

### Step 5 — Maintain the Relationship
Partners who feel well-served refer more.
- Monthly update (same quality as investor reports)
- Quarterly check-in call
- Recognition when a referral converts and performs well

## Referral Compensation Framework

**Standard referral:** One-time commission or ongoing revenue share on AUM introduced
- Specific terms set case by case with JorDache approval
- Never promise what hasn't been agreed upon internally
- Document every referral arrangement before introductions begin

**Non-monetary value exchange:**
- Access to Blue Marlin research publications
- Priority communication and reporting
- Event and meetup invitations
- Introduction to the Blue Marlin network (reciprocal value)

## Institutional Outreach Approach

For family offices and institutional intermediaries:

**Never cold pitch.** The approach is:

1. Build awareness through Blue Marlin's content, website, and research publications
2. Get a warm introduction through an existing network connection
3. First contact is relationship-only — no pitch, no materials unless asked
4. Allow the relationship to develop over multiple interactions
5. Present materials only when the institutional party requests them

**Timeline acceptance:** Institutional capital relationships take time.
A 12-month relationship that results in a $500K allocation is worth far more than
a rushed $20K allocation from a misaligned investor.

## Capital Raising Event Strategy

### Private Investor Dinner / Roundtable
- Format: 8–12 qualified guests. Dinner or private lounge setting.
- Host: JorDache and Sultan
- Content: 20-minute strategy overview, open discussion, relationship building
- Outcome: 2–4 qualified follow-up calls per event
- Frequency: Quarterly minimum when in key markets (Bahamas, Dubai, Singapore, London)

### Webinar (Semi-Private)
- Format: Invitation-only, maximum 50 attendees
- Content: Full Blue Marlin strategy presentation + Q&A
- Gate: Application or introduction required to attend
- Outcome: CRM pipeline additions + qualified discovery call bookings

### Conference Appearances
- JorDache speaking or networking at finance, entrepreneurship, or family office events
- Blue Marlin materials available on request — never handed out unsolicited
- Outcome: Warm introductions and brand credibility, not direct conversion

## AUM Growth Targets (9-Month Plan Context)

The 9-Month Operator Wealth Plan (May 2026 – February 2027) targets AUM expansion as a
core objective. Capital raising activities should be sequenced to support this trajectory.

**Priority order for capital raising focus:**
1. Warm network and existing community (fastest to convert)
2. Referral partner development (medium timeline, high leverage)
3. Institutional introductions (long timeline, highest value per relationship)
4. Content-driven inbound (compound over time, lowest effort per lead)

**Never sacrifice trust for speed.** A smaller AUM built on properly qualified, fully informed
investors creates a more stable and referable capital base than a large AUM built on misaligned expectations.
