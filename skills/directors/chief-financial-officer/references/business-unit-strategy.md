# Business Unit Financial Strategy

## Financial Overview by Brand

Each business unit has a distinct financial profile, priority focus, and optimization lever.

---

## 4x Heist Investments (Parent / Holding Company)

**Financial role:** Capital consolidator. Funds other units. Receives management fees and distributions.

**Revenue:** Management fees from subsidiaries, investment returns, future equity distributions

**Financial priority:** Build the holding structure properly. Every sub-brand should eventually
operate as a standalone P&L that reports up to 4x Heist.

**Key metric:** Total enterprise value across all brands

**Current focus:** Ensure all revenue streams are properly attributed to the right entity.
No commingling. Clean books enable future sale, investment, or partnership conversations.

## 4x Prophet

**Financial role:** Primary revenue engine and attention acquisition layer

**Revenue mix:**
- Copy trading subscriptions: $267/month recurring
- Operator Mentorship: $5,000/month (high-ticket, variable volume)
- Courses and digital products: one-time
- Affiliate commissions: prop firm codes, broker deal ($8/lot)
- Alpha Capital retainer + funded account ($200K)

The Operator Mentorship is high-revenue but variable — build the copy trading base to create
a stable floor that the mentorship sits on top of.

**Optimization lever:** Increase mentorship seat utilization. Each additional mentorship client
at $5,000/month adds $60,000/year to top line. Target: [X] seats filled at any given time.

**Key metrics:** MRR / Mentorship seats active / Affiliate commission monthly / LTV by tier

**Warning signals:** Churn rate above 8% monthly / Mentorship pipeline empty / Affiliate commissions declining

## HowToBeBullish (HTBB)

**Financial role:** Recurring revenue community — medium ticket, high volume

**Revenue mix:**
- Monthly memberships: $177/month
- Semi-annual: $997
- Annual: $1,497/year
- Co-founder split: JorDache and Noah Bush (confirm terms)

**Financial priority:** Maximize annual plan conversion. An annual subscriber at $1,497 is worth
8.5x a monthly subscriber and creates no churn risk for 12 months.

**Optimization lever:** Discount incentive for annual upgrade at 30/60/90 day mark.
Annual plan at $1,497 vs. $177 × 12 = $2,124 — annual is already 30% cheaper for the member.
Make this math visible.

**Key metrics:** MRR / Annual vs. monthly split / Churn rate / New member CAC / Funded trader count

**Unit economics target:** LTV:CAC > 4:1

## Blue Marlin Securities

**Financial role:** Capital and performance business — highest revenue per client, lowest volume

**Revenue mix:**
- Performance fees: 30% of profits (algo) / 50% of profits (manual)
- Management fees: 5% annualized / 12 months (algo only)
- Minimum AUM per account: $7,500

**Financial priority:** AUM growth is the compounding lever. Every additional $100,000 AUM
at 5% management fee = $5,000/year guaranteed. Performance fees compound on top.

**AUM growth math:**
```
$100K AUM at 5% mgmt + 30% performance on 15% monthly return:
  Management:   $5,000/year ($417/month)
  Performance:  30% × $15,000 = $4,500/month (if performance holds)
  Total:        ~$54,000/year per $100K AUM at consistent performance
```

**Optimization lever:** Increase minimum allocation conversation. A $7,500 account generates
minimal fee revenue. The priority is moving investors toward $25,000–$100,000+ allocations.

**Key metrics:** Total AUM / Average allocation size / Number of active accounts / Fee revenue monthly

## Legacy By Darling

**Financial role:** Premium coaching — high-ticket, low volume, high LTV

**Revenue:** $2,000/month, 3-month minimum = $6,000 minimum per client

**Financial priority:** Retention and transformation. A client who achieves results refers.
Three referrals per year from transformed clients = $18,000 in additional revenue per referral client.

**Optimization lever:** Build the referral engine. Every successful transformation is a marketing asset.

**Key metrics:** Active clients / Monthly revenue / Average client duration / Referral rate

**Note:** Legacy By Darling is strictly separated from trading content. Financial reporting
for this brand keeps a completely distinct P&L.

## MrKillDaMarkets

**Revenue:** Affiliate commissions, content monetization, audience driving to paid products

**Financial priority:** Minimize direct costs. This brand should be lean — its value is

**Key metric:** Cost per lead generated for 4x Prophet and HTBB (attributed revenue vs. content costs)

## Cross-Brand Financial Optimization

### Revenue Concentration Risk
No single brand should represent more than 60% of total revenue.
Current: 4x Prophet likely dominates. Priority: build HTBB recurring and Blue Marlin AUM
to reduce single-brand dependency.

### Shared Cost Optimization
Identify expenses serving multiple brands (software, design, automation) and allocate properly.
One Supabase database, one N8N instance, one design system — serving all brands.
Build shared infrastructure that reduces total cost as revenue scales.

### Tax Structure
Holding company structure enables income to be recognized at the entity level.
Da Nang / Bali residency creates favorable tax positioning.
Bahamas relocation (target milestone) creates further tax efficiency.
Ensure accountant/attorney familiar with multi-jurisdiction digital business structure.
