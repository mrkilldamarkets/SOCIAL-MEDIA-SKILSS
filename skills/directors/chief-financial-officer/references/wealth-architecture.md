# Wealth Architecture — Personal Wealth, Forecasting & 40 Before 30

## Personal Wealth Framework

The business generates revenue. The wealth architecture converts that revenue into permanent assets.

```
Step 1 — Income
  Generate from multiple streams. Never rely on one.
  Current: trading, memberships, mentorship, affiliates, asset management, content.

Step 2 — Cash Flow
  Convert income into reliable monthly surplus.
  Target: surplus after all business and personal expenses.
  Measure: monthly net positive delta.

Step 3 — Assets
  Deploy surplus into owned assets.
  Not consumption. Not lifestyle. Owned, appreciating or cash-flowing assets.
  Priority assets: real estate (Bahamas Unit 501 target), media IP, software equity.

Step 4 — Investments
  From asset surplus — allocate to external investments.
  Only after business and personal assets are secured.
  Filter: cash flow generating or high-conviction appreciation.

Step 5 — Freedom
  The point at which passive cash flow covers all personal expenses.
  This is the financial milestone, not a lifestyle achievement.
  Measure: passive income / monthly personal burn rate.

Step 6 — Legacy
  Structures that persist beyond the founder.
  Trusts, entities, insurance, family infrastructure.
  Plan for this in the 30s — not as an afterthought.
```

## Forecasting Framework

Run all four time horizons minimum monthly:

### 30-Day Forecast
- Revenue: confirmed recurring + expected variable + pipeline probability
- Expenses: fixed + committed variable
- Net: expected cash position at month-end
- Actions required if below target

### 90-Day Forecast
- Revenue trend projection by stream
- Expected new revenue (product launches, mentorship conversions, AUM additions)
- Capital allocation plan for the quarter
- Reserve position at quarter-end

### 6-Month Forecast
- Business unit performance projections
- AUM growth trajectory (Blue Marlin)
- Recurring revenue growth trajectory (HTBB, 4x Prophet)
- Capital available for asset acquisition
- Progress toward Bahamas milestone

### 12-Month Forecast
- Total revenue target by brand
- Net worth projection
- Asset acquisition plan
- Financial freedom progress (passive income vs. burn rate)
- Holding company enterprise value estimate

### 3-Year Vision
- Target AUM under management
- Target recurring revenue base
- Real estate and asset portfolio value
- Bahamas infrastructure established
- Personal wealth milestone (net worth target)

### 10-Year Vision
- Multi-generational wealth infrastructure
- Holding company matured across all seven brands
- Legacy structures in place
- Capital allocation operating with minimal founder dependency
