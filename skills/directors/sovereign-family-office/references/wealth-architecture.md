# Wealth Architecture — Assets, Allocation & Preservation

## Asset Accumulation Priority Order

Every surplus dollar is evaluated against this hierarchy before allocation:

```
1. Operating Business Reinvestment (highest leverage — builds enterprise value)
2. Cash Flow Assets (income-producing without active management)
3. Real Estate (Bahamas primary — family infrastructure + appreciation)
4. Investment Vehicles (private equity, minority stakes, structured products)
5. Liquid Reserves (capital preservation — not opportunity capital)
6. Education / Knowledge Assets (intellectual property, systems, processes)
```

**Never:** Consumption spending from the family office capital pool.
Personal lifestyle is funded from operating income — not family office assets.

## Wealth Preservation Framework

**Five threats to generational wealth — in order of probability:**

1. **Lifestyle inflation** — The most common destroyer. Income grows, so spending grows.
   Protection: personal lifestyle budget is capped as a percentage of total income, not as a feel.

2. **Poor capital allocation** — Investing in ego projects, low-margin businesses, or vanity assets.
   Protection: every capital deployment above $5,000 runs the acquisition/allocation filter.

3. **Concentration risk** — Too much wealth in one asset, one business, or one market.
   Protection: diversify across operating companies, media, real estate, and liquid reserves.

4. **Emotional decisions** — Panic selling, euphoric buying, revenge spending after a loss.
   Protection: a 48-hour rule on all non-urgent capital decisions above $10,000.

5. **Generational transfer failure** — Wealth destroyed by unprepared heirs.
   Protection: the family governance and education systems (see legacy-systems.md).

## Capital Allocation for the Family Office

**Every dollar has a mission:**

| Mission | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Compound the asset base | Reinvest into operating businesses |
| Preservation | Protect existing wealth | Liquid reserves, hard assets |
| Opportunity | Deploy on high-conviction targets | M&A, private investments |
| Education | Build family capability | Knowledge, systems, training |
| Infrastructure | Family quality of life | Primary residence, health |
| Legacy | Multi-generational systems | Trusts, succession structures |

**Allocation rule:** Growth and Preservation always funded first.
Opportunity and Legacy capital only deployed from surplus above those two.

## Investment Philosophy

**Invest in:** Businesses / Systems / Media assets / Software / Intellectual property /
Brand equity / Cash flow assets / People (selective equity partnerships)

**Investment filter for any external opportunity:**
1. Does this generate cash flow within 24 months?
2. Is the downside survivable without restructuring the family office?
3. Is this in a domain where we have genuine insight — or are we speculating?
4. Does this concentrate risk in an area already heavily weighted?
5. Would we still own this in ten years under adverse conditions?

**One no on questions 1, 2, or 3 → do not invest.**
