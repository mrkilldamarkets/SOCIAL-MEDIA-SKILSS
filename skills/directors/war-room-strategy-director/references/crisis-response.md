# Crisis Response Frameworks — By Crisis Type

## Universal Response Protocol

Regardless of crisis type, the first 60 minutes follow this sequence:

```
MINUTE 0–15:  ASSESS
  → What exactly happened? (Facts only — no speculation)
  → Who is affected? (People, operations, capital, reputation)
  → Is this still escalating or contained?
  → What do we know? What don't we know?

MINUTE 15–30: PRIORITIZE
  → Which of the five crisis priorities is most threatened?
  → What action in the next 24 hours prevents the worst outcome?
  → What decisions can wait 24 hours without making things worse?

MINUTE 30–45: COMMUNICATE
  → Who needs to know right now? (Investors, clients, team, community)
  → What do we say? (Facts we know + what we're doing + what we don't yet know)
  → What do we NOT say? (Speculation, blame, premature conclusions)

MINUTE 45–60: RESPOND
  → First action taken
  → Clear owner for each subsequent action
  → Next check-in time defined
```

## Financial Crisis Response

**Triggers:** Revenue drops >30% in a month / Cash reserves below 1.5 months / Investor mass withdrawal / Unexpected major expense

```
HOUR 0–24: STABILIZE
  □ Full financial audit: current cash, burn rate, exact runway
  □ Revenue triage: which streams are down and why?
  □ Immediate expense reduction: pause all non-essential spending
  □ Do not make new capital commitments until runway is understood

DAY 1–7: PROTECT CORE
  □ Identify the 20% of activities generating 80% of revenue — protect those only
  □ All expansion plans suspended
  □ Communicate with any investors or creditors who may be affected
  □ Evaluate which team/contractor costs can be temporarily reduced

DAY 7–30: STABILIZE REVENUE
  □ Increase outbound activity on highest-margin, fastest-close offers
  □ Operator Mentorship and Blue Marlin investor calls — direct outreach
  □ Evaluate whether the decline is cyclical or structural
  □ If structural: major strategic review required before growth is attempted

ONGOING: EXTEND RUNWAY
  □ Target: 6-month reserve minimum before resuming growth initiatives
  □ No new hires, no new product launches, no new brand initiatives until stable
```

## Brand / Reputation Crisis Response

**Triggers:** Public negative coverage / Community backlash / False claims circulating / Partnership fallout / Content misrepresentation

```
FIRST 2 HOURS: GATHER FACTS
  □ What exactly was said, by whom, where?
  □ Is this accurate, partially accurate, or false?
  □ How widely is it spreading?
  □ Who are the affected parties?

RESPONSE DECISION:
  Tier 1 — Minor noise, limited reach: Monitor only. Do not engage.
  (Engaging amplifies. Silence often extinguishes minor situations.)

  Tier 2 — Substantive criticism, growing reach: One clear, factual response.
  Format: Acknowledge / Correct the record / Describe action taken
  Never: Argue / Attack back / Over-explain / Apologize for things not done wrong

  Tier 3 — Serious reputation threat, significant reach: Controlled, coordinated response.
  → Legal counsel consulted before any public statement
  → Single public statement — factual, measured, final
  → No social media debates
  → Investor and key client communication sent privately before public response

RECOVERY:
  □ Increase positive evidence output (results, testimonials, process documentation)
  □ Platform activity continues normally — silence post-crisis looks like guilt
  □ Do not over-reference the incident — move forward with standard positioning
  □ Document what happened and what response was effective
```

## Platform Crisis Response

**Triggers:** Instagram/YouTube ban, Discord failure, Telegram restrictions, website outage, tech failure

```
WITHIN 1 HOUR:
  □ Confirm whether ban/outage is temporary or permanent
  □ Activate backup communication channel (priority: Telegram → Email → X)
  □ Post a brief, calm status update to affected audience: "We're aware of the issue. Follow [backup channel] for updates."

WITHIN 24 HOURS:
  □ Appeal the ban if applicable (YouTube: appeal immediately, do not wait)
  □ Migrate core content access to backup platform
  □ Notify HTBB/4x Prophet community of the alternative access path

WITHIN 72 HOURS:
  □ Assess whether restoration is likely or whether pivot is required
  □ If permanent ban confirmed: begin rebuilding on primary backup platform immediately
  □ Update all links, bios, and resources to reflect backup platform

PREVENTION GOING FORWARD:
  □ Email list is the owned audience — grow it aggressively (no platform can ban email)
  □ Telegram is the primary backup for all social platforms
  □ Never rely on a single platform for more than 50% of audience access
```

## Investor Crisis Response (Blue Marlin)

**Triggers:** Significant drawdown, investor anxiety, withdrawal requests, market volatility creating fear

```
WHEN DRAWDOWN EXCEEDS 10%:
  → Proactive communication BEFORE the monthly report (see IR skill for template)
  → Facts only: what happened, why, current status, what changes
  → No spin, no manufactured positivity

WITHDRAWAL REQUESTS:
  → Acknowledge immediately: "Received. Processing per our agreement."
  → Confirm timeline: standard withdrawal process via BlackBull
  → No pushback, no pressure to stay — respect the decision without drama
  → Internal review: is this one investor or a signal of broader anxiety?

MARKET STRESS COMMUNICATION:
  → Monthly report frequency may increase to bi-weekly during high volatility
  → Host an investor Q&A call if multiple investors are expressing concerns
  → Never go silent — silence amplifies fear

AFTER WITHDRAWALS:
  → Evaluate whether portfolio changes are required
  → Use the event to build better communication protocols for next time
  → Every managed withdrawal handled well is a reference for future investors
```

## Decision-Making Under Pressure

When time is short and information is incomplete:

```
PRESSURE DECISION FRAMEWORK:

What matters most right now?         [Primary priority — focus only on this]
What is urgent?                      [Must happen in hours — not days]
What is important but can wait?      [24–48 hours without disaster]
What is reversible?                  [Can be undone if wrong]
What is irreversible?                [Must be right before acting]

RULE: Only make irreversible decisions when the situation is clear.
     When in doubt on an irreversible decision — wait.
     When in doubt on a reversible decision — act and adjust.
```

## Post-Crisis Review (Run Within 72 Hours of Resolution)

```
POST-CRISIS REVIEW:

What happened?              [Timeline of events — factual]
What caused it?             [Root cause — not surface cause]
What did we do well?        [Response that worked]
What should have been different? [Honest assessment]
What system failed?         [The upstream cause of the crisis]
What system is now stronger? [Improvement made]
Lessons documented?         [Y/N — must be written, not just discussed]
Update to contingency plan? [Y/N — which section]
```
