## Red Team Analysis Framework

Run quarterly — or immediately before any major decision, launch, or expansion.

**Red team objective:** Find every way the plan can fail before committing resources.

```
RED TEAM SESSION:

Plan being tested:      [Specific plan, strategy, or decision]
Red team question:      "How does this fail?"

Round 1 — External failure:
  → What market conditions make this not work?
  → What competitor action breaks this?
  → What regulatory or platform change kills this?

Round 2 — Internal failure:
  → What execution errors could destroy this?
  → What assumptions are we making that might be wrong?
  → What happens if the key person for this leaves?

Round 3 — Timing failure:
  → What if this takes 3x longer than planned?
  → What if it costs 2x more than budgeted?
  → What if the first attempt fails — is there a second?

Round 4 — Cascade failure:
  → If this fails, what else fails with it?
  → What dependencies does this create that didn't exist before?

OUTPUT:
  Top 3 failure modes:   [Ranked by severity and probability]
  Mitigations applied:   [What changes to the plan as a result]
  Go / No-go decision:   [Proceed / Modify / Abort — with rationale]
```

## Black Swan Preparation

Black swans cannot be predicted — they can be survived through resilience infrastructure.

**The three black swan responses:**

**Financial black swan** (global recession, market crash, major client/investor fraud):
- 6-month reserve minimum protects runway
- No single investor representing >30% of AUM (Blue Marlin)
- No single revenue stream representing >60% of income
- Lean operational structure that can contract 40% rapidly if needed

**Technology black swan** (internet infrastructure failure, major platform shutdown, AI regulation):
- Offline content library (courses, materials downloadable by clients)
- Email-first communication protocol can operate without social platforms
- Manual processes documented for all critical workflows (not just automated)

**Reputation black swan** (viral misinformation, coordinated attack, partnership fraud):
- Legal retainer in place for rapid response
- Clear factual record of all claims, results, and processes
- Positive evidence library maintained and current
- Community trust built over time — the buffer against isolated attacks

**The fundamental black swan posture:**
Do not optimize so tightly that there is no room to absorb a shock.
Efficiency is valuable. Resilience is essential. Sacrifice some efficiency to maintain resilience.
