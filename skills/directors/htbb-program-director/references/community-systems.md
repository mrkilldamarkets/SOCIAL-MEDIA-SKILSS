# Community Systems — Discord Architecture & Culture

## Core Principle

The community is the product. Not the Discord server. Not the education. Not the calls.

The environment is what transforms people. Protect it at all costs.

## Community Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **No signals.** Share setups with full thesis or don't share.
2. **No hype.** "To the moon" energy has no home here.
3. **No guaranteed outcomes.** Never promise someone a result.
4. **No negativity spirals.** One loss does not define the week. Correct and move.
5. **Accountability only.** Complaints without action are not tolerated.
6. **Respect execution.** Someone who takes a loss with good process is more respected than someone who won by luck.
7. **No self-promotion.** No promoting other programs, communities, or services.
8. **Protect the culture.** If something feels off — flag it. Culture is everyone's responsibility.

**Violations:** Warning → Removal. No second chances for serious breaches (hype, scams, negative influence).

| 90-day journal streak | "Discipline Award" + feature in weekly call |
| Phase 04 Operator Status | "HTBB Operator" role — permanent recognition |
| HTBB Legend | Nomination-based. Rare. Reserved for outstanding transformation stories. |

**Recognition philosophy:** Celebrate the process as loudly as the result.
A funded pass matters. A 90-day journal streak matters just as much.

## Culture Protection Protocol

The culture degrades silently before it collapses publicly. Watch for early signals.

**Warning signs:**
- Members sharing results without context (hype without process)
- Complaints about market conditions without self-reflection
- "When will we get signals?" energy
- Comparisons to other communities or gurus
- Members who only appear after big wins and disappear during drawdowns

**Response to culture drift:**
1. Address it directly in #announcements — not confrontationally, but clearly
2. Double down on accountability content
3. Feature a process-first transformation story within the week
4. If a member is the source — private warning first, removal if repeated
