# Funnel Architecture — Funnels, Lead Magnets & Email

## Customer Journey Map

Every marketing system guides prospects through this sequence:

```
AWARENESS     → They discover the brand (content, ads, referral, search)
INTEREST      → They engage with the content or offer
EDUCATION     → They begin to understand the problem and the solution
TRUST         → They believe this could work and that this source is credible
APPLICATION   → They take the first committed action (DM, form, booking)
CONVERSION    → Sale completed / access granted
RETENTION     → They achieve results and want to stay
ADVOCACY      → They refer others because the outcome was real
```

## Lead Magnet Philosophy

Lead magnets provide: Immediate value / Quick wins / Trust / Credibility / Next step direction

**Effective lead magnet formats:**

| Format | Best For | Example |
|---|---|---|
| PDF Guide | Education-led audience | "The Operator Trading System — 7 Frameworks for Consistent Execution" |
| Checklist | Action-oriented audience | "Prop Firm Challenge Preparation Checklist" |
| Video Training | High-intent prospects | "How I Built a Funded Trading Income from Zero" |
| Email Mini-Course | Nurture-heavy audiences | "5 Days to Your First Funded Account — The Real Process" |
| Free Template | Tool-oriented audience | "Weekly Trading Bias Framework Template" |

**Lead magnet rules:**
- Solve one specific problem completely — don't give partial answers hoping they buy the rest
- The lead magnet should make them want more — not feel satisfied and leave
- Always include a natural next step at the end (join the community / book a call / apply)

## Copywriting Framework (PAE-SPA)

**Problem → Agitation → Education → Solution → Proof → Action**

```
PROBLEM (1–2 sentences):
  State the problem in the prospect's exact language.
  "You're consistently inconsistent. Some months you make money. Most months you don't."

AGITATION (2–3 sentences):
  Explore the cost of the problem. Not manufactured drama — real consequences.
  "Every inconsistent month costs you more than money. It costs confidence, credibility,
  and the belief that this is even possible for you."

EDUCATION (3–5 sentences):
  Why does the problem exist? What's the root cause most people miss?
  "The problem isn't your strategy. Inconsistency is almost always a systems problem —
  specifically, the absence of a repeatable process that doesn't depend on motivation."

SOLUTION (2–3 sentences):
  Introduce the offer as the answer to the root cause.
  "That's exactly what [program] is designed to solve. Not just better trades —
  a system that creates consistent operators."

PROOF (2–5 sentences or testimonials):
  Evidence that the solution works. Specific, believable, transformation-focused.
  "[Name] joined with no prop firm funding. 90 days later, they passed their first challenge.
  [Name] went from three losing months in a row to their first funded withdrawal."

ACTION (1–2 sentences):
  One clear step. No multiple choices.
  "If this sounds like what you've been looking for — [specific action]."
```

## Email Marketing Sequences

### New Lead Welcome Sequence (7 emails over 14 days)

```
Email 1 (Immediate): Welcome + deliver the lead magnet + set expectations
Email 2 (Day 2):     The problem in depth — agitate the root cause
Email 3 (Day 4):     The solution framework — education-heavy, no pitch
Email 4 (Day 6):     A transformation story — specific member or client result
Email 5 (Day 8):     Objections addressed — time, money, trust, past failure
Email 6 (Day 11):    The offer — direct, clear, one CTA
Email 7 (Day 14):    Final — why the decision matters, last CTA
```

**Email copy rules:**
- Subject lines: curiosity or clear benefit — never clickbait
- Open with the most interesting sentence (no "I hope this email finds you well")
- One CTA per email — at the bottom
- Plain text or minimal HTML — not designed newsletter format for sequence emails
- Mobile-optimized: short paragraphs, frequent line breaks

## Landing Page Structure

```
HERO SECTION:
  Headline:     Who this is for + the transformation (not the product name)
  Subheadline:  Clarify the mechanism and the outcome
  CTA:          Primary action (apply, join, book, download)
  Visual:       High-quality, brand-standard, no stock photos

PROBLEM SECTION:
  Agitate the problem. Speak their language. Make them feel seen.

SOLUTION SECTION:
  Introduce the program. Focus on outcome, not features.

PROOF SECTION:
  Testimonials: Transformation-focused. Journey from X to Y.
  Case studies: Specific results with context.

OFFER SECTION:
  What's included. Clear and complete. No jargon.
  Price (if public). Framing: investment, not expense.

OBJECTION SECTION:
  FAQ format. Address top 5–7 objections honestly.

FINAL CTA:
  Same as hero CTA. Repeat the transformation.
  Deadline or next cohort date if applicable and real.
```
