## Capital Introduction Partnership Development

**Target partner profiles:**

**Wealth Advisors / IFAs:**
- Serve HNW clients who already trust them to direct capital
- Blue Marlin offers a managed account alternative allocation
- Relationship requirements: highest documentation standards, clear regulatory compliance
- Timeline to first introduction: 3–12 months of relationship building

**Family Office Intermediaries:**
- Access to ultra-HNW and institutional capital
- Require institutional-grade documentation, track record, and operational infrastructure
- Timeline: 6–18 months from first contact to capital introduction
- Worth every hour invested — one family office relationship changes the AUM curve

**Business Networks and Entrepreneur Communities:**
- HNW individuals with capital to deploy outside core business
- Decision cycle faster than institutional — 1–3 months from introduction to investment
- Access through: mastermind networks, operator communities, entrepreneurship events

## Conference & Event Strategy

**Prioritize events that provide:** Institutional access / Capital access / Partnership opportunities / Industry intelligence / Long-term relationships

**Target event categories:**
- Investment management conferences (alternatives, managed accounts, family office)
- Fintech and trading infrastructure conferences
- Private wealth and family office summits
- Regional financial industry events in priority markets (Dubai, Singapore, London, Bahamas)

**Conference preparation protocol:**

```
PRE-EVENT (2 weeks before):
  □ Research attendee list — identify top 10 targets for meetings
  □ Prepare meeting request outreach for top targets
  □ Review latest Blue Marlin performance data for conversational use
  □ Prepare institutional materials: IIM summary, one-pager, business cards
  □ Set meeting schedule: 4–6 pre-booked meetings per day maximum

AT EVENT:
  □ Lead with relationship — not pitch
  □ Ask questions before presenting anything
  □ Exchange contact information and agree specific next step before leaving each conversation
  □ Notes taken within 2 hours of each conversation

POST-EVENT (48 hours):
  □ All contacts added to CRM with meeting notes
  □ Follow-up email sent to each meaningful contact: specific reference to conversation + one clear next step
  □ Priority contacts escalated for discovery call scheduling
```
