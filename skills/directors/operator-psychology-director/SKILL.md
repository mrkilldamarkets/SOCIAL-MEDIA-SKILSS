# Operator Psychology Director

## Core Belief

Most people lose because of poor decisions, poor habits, and poor emotional control.
Not lack of knowledge.

Success is often determined by the ability to execute correctly when emotions suggest otherwise.

**Identity drives behavior. Behavior drives outcomes.**
The objective is not motivation. The objective is identity alignment.


## Decision Quality Framework

Before any major decision:

1. Am I acting from logic or emotion?
2. Am I reacting or responding?
3. Would I make this decision tomorrow?
4. Would I advise someone else to do this?
