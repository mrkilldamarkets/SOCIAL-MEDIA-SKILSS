# Location Intelligence — Market Evaluation & Operations

## Market Evaluation Framework

Every potential base of operations or market evaluated against eight criteria:

```
LOCATION ASSESSMENT — [City/Country]

INFRASTRUCTURE:
  Internet quality:         [Speed, reliability, fiber availability]
  Power reliability:        [Stable / frequent outages]
  Co-working / workspace:   [Quality options available?]
  Banking:                  [International banking access, ease of transfers]

LIFESTYLE:
  Safety:                   [General safety level for foreigner operator]
  Healthcare:                [Quality and accessibility]
  Fitness:                  [Quality gym access]
  Food:                     [Nutrition-compatible options readily available]
  Social environment:        [Community of operators / builders / entrepreneurs?]

CONTENT PRODUCTION:
  Visual settings:          [Cinematic locations for content — architecture, nature, urban]
  Light quality:            [Hours of golden light, climate]
  Travel connectivity:      [Airport access, flight connections to key cities]

BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT:
  Visa/residency options:   [Length of stay, business visa options]
  Tax environment:          [Local tax on foreign income — if any]
  Legal infrastructure:     [Contract enforcement, business registration]

COST STRUCTURE:
  Monthly living cost:      $[X] for operator standard of living
  Accommodation:            $[X]/month (quality serviced apartment or house)
  Gym/fitness:              $[X]/month
  Food:                     $[X]/month

STRATEGIC VALUE:
  Network:                  [Proximity to investors, partners, or target audience]
  Content angle:            [Does being here generate unique content value?]
  Business development:     [Can meaningful business meetings happen here?]

OVERALL RATING:             [Strong base / Operational base / Short-term only / Avoid]
```

## Active Location Intelligence

- Affordable, high-speed internet, growing expat community
- Good gym options, clean city, safe
- Strong for content production (beaches, urban, mountain proximity)
- Visa: e-visa and tourist visa rotation (not long-term residency)
- Monthly cost: Lean operator budget ~$2,000–$2,500/month

### Bali, Indonesia (June 2026 — Next Base)
- Strong entrepreneur and operator community (Canggu, Ubud)
- High-competition environment — fitness, lifestyle, business energy
- Good content backdrop (nature, architecture, lifestyle)
- Coworking infrastructure strong
- Monthly cost: $2,500–$4,000/month at quality level
- Visa: social visa / business visit (no long-term residency without entity)
- Strategic value: network access to operators, creators, and entrepreneurs; proximity to Singapore for banking/institutional meetings

### Bahamas (Long-Term Target)
- Primary family base / legacy anchor
- No income tax, capital gains tax, or inheritance tax on non-Bahamian income
- Stable political environment, English-speaking, Caribbean lifestyle
- Atlantis Unit 501 (Gregory Moss buyer — negotiation in prior history)
- Monthly cost: Higher than Asia but offset by tax efficiency
- Strategic value: home base / tax residency / family infrastructure / investor meetings (Caribbean and US proximity)

- No income tax for residents
- Strong HNW investor and family office network
- Access to Middle Eastern capital and institutional players

## Travel Infrastructure for Operators

For a location-independent operator running a global business:

**Non-negotiables in any location:**
- Minimum 100 Mbps fiber internet (dedicated, not shared hotel WiFi)
- 24/7 gym access or 5-day minimum
- Airport within 2 hours (international connections available)
- Food options that support macro targets without constant meal prep difficulty

**Pack-out standard:**
- MacBook Pro + portable monitor
- Rode Wireless ME lavalier mic (always ready to film)
- DJI Pocket or equivalent camera for B-roll capture
- Hard drive for content library backup
- Travel router (backup internet redundancy)
- Portable keyboard / mouse for extended work sessions
